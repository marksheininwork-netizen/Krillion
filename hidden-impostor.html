🦎 Hidden Impostor

A secret-word deduction party game. Everyone gets the word except one hidden impostor. Give clues, discuss, and vote.

Set up a game

Enter 3–12 names, separated by commas. Pass one device between players.

Local pass-and-play. Online rooms are not yet supported.

Hand the device to the named player. No peeking!

Clue phase

Everyone gives one clue

Take turns saying a short clue related to the secret word. The impostor must blend in without knowing it.

Who is the impostor?

Select one person. Votes are secret until everyone has voted.
